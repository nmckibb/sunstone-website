## How to apply and pay

1. Apply on RentRedi — the $50 application includes a criminal and background check.
2. Screening — every guest is screened on behavior; current guests' comfort comes first.
3. Pay first month — once approved, pay your first month's rent through RentRedi: Zelle, PayPal, or bank deposit. That's all that's due up front.
4. Move in — 30-night minimum stay. Book 31+ days to skip the short-term lodging taxes entirely.
