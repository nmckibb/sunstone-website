Terms & house rules

A plain-language summary of staying at Sunstone House. Private-contract guests also sign a monthly stay agreement, which governs the stay; Airbnb bookings follow Airbnb’s terms as well.

Stays

  • 31-day minimum stay; month-to-month after that.
  • Monthly rates include utilities. Book 31 days or more and the Portland / Multnomah short-term lodging taxes don’t apply.
  • Stays are for working professionals in a quiet shared household.

Rates & payment

  • No deposit on private contracts through RentRedi.
  • Apply on RentRedi — the $50 application includes a criminal and background check, plus behavior screening.
  • Once approved, pay your first month’s rent through RentRedi: Zelle, PayPal, or bank deposit.
  • Tenants are responsible for any damage to the room.

House rules

  • Quiet hours 10 PM–7 AM, and low noise at all hours.
  • No smoking or vaping anywhere on the property, including marijuana.
  • No pets, no extra furniture.
  • No unregistered guests; no parties or events.
  • Clean up after yourself in shared areas and bathrooms; label food with your room number.
  • Keep doors secured; park only where directed.

Cancellations

Airbnb bookings follow Airbnb’s cancellation terms for the listing. Private-contract stays follow the signed monthly stay agreement. If plans change, tell us as early as you can.

Address & safety

For the safety of current guests, the street address is shared only after booking. The house is in Kenton, North Portland, OR 97217.

Questions? Contact us.
