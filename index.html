The house

A quiet, eco-conscious home in Kenton, on the Arbor Lodge border — built for people who work, study, and need rest.

Amenities

  • Fast Wi-Fi throughout
  • Full shared kitchen
  • Keyless entry, private rooms
  • Desk & workspace in most rooms
  • Free in-unit washer & dryer
  • Free off-street parking
  • Backyard, patio & edible garden
  • 50+ amenities, utilities included

Getting around

TriMet Line 35 stops about 4 blocks away and runs to South Waterfront and the Rose Quarter. About 1 mile to the University of Portland, 2 miles to Adidas, I-5 about a mile away — and seven hospitals within a 10–20 minute drive.

House rules, in short

Quiet hours 10 PM–7 AM. No parties, no overnight guests, no smoking, no pets. The house rules are based on respect for people sleeping down the hall.

Why “Sunstone”?

Sunstone is Oregon's state gem. Nathan loves gems and Shelley creates jewelry herself, so the house took the gem's name — and every room is named for a gemstone too: Opal, Sapphire, Emerald, Garnet, and the rest. Small stones, each one different, all under one roof.
