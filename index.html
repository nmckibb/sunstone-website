About Sunstone House

Sunstone House offers private furnished bedrooms in a shared home in Portland's Kenton neighborhood. Guests get their own room and share the kitchen and common spaces with a small number of other monthly guests — a quieter, more settled alternative to short-term rentals.

Stays are monthly, which keeps the house calm and the community stable. Most guests are in Portland for work assignments, contracts, or relocation.

What's included

  • Utilities & Wi-Fi includedOne monthly price — no separate bills.
  • Shared kitchen & common areasFull kitchen, living spaces, and laundry access.
  • No depositSimple monthly terms for stays of a month or longer.

Monthly stays

Rooms run $1,100–$1,350 per month depending on the room, with utilities included. Stays are month-to-month after an initial term, making Sunstone a good fit for:

  • Travel nurses and medical professionals on assignment
  • Contractors and remote workers on longer projects
  • People relocating to Portland who want time to find a permanent place

Please note: Sunstone House is a no-pets home.
