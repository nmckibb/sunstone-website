The house

A quiet, eco-conscious home in Kenton, on the Arbor Lodge border — built for people who work, study, and need real rest.

  • Fast Wi-Fi throughout
  • Free in-unit washer & dryer
  • Full shared kitchen
  • Free off-street parking
  • Keyless entry, private rooms
  • Backyard, patio & edible garden
  • Desk & workspace in most rooms
  • 50+ amenities, utilities included

Getting around

TriMet Line 35 stops about 4 blocks away and runs straight to South Waterfront and the Rose Quarter. About 1 mile to the University of Portland, 2 miles to Adidas, I-5 about a mile away, and seven hospitals within a 10–20 minute drive.

House rules, in short

Quiet hours 10 PM–7 AM. No parties, no overnight guests, no smoking. Every rule in the house book is there because the house runs on respect for the people sleeping down the hall.

Why “Sunstone”?

The sunstone is Oregon's state gem. Nathan loves gems, and Shelley creates jewelry herself — so the house took the gem's name, and every room in it is named for a gemstone too: Opal, Sapphire, Emerald, Garnet, and the rest. Small stones, each one different, all under one roof.
